# Pebrel Winc 2.5.6

## English

### Added

- Added per-group startup directories for local and WSL terminals. Right-click a tab group and select **Set startup directory**. Choose a tab in the group to fill in its current path and, for WSL, its distribution. The group's **+** preselects a compatible terminal and shows the saved path. The setting is kept across restarts, even when the group has no tabs.

## 简体中文

### 新增

- 标签分组支持为本机和 WSL 终端设置启动目录。右键分组并选择「设置启动目录」，选取组内标签即可自动填入当前路径，WSL 标签还会填入发行版。点击分组「＋」会预选匹配的终端并显示已保存的路径。即使分组没有标签，设置也会在重启后保留。
