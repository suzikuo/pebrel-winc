# Pebrel Winc 2.5.10

## English

### Added

- Added direct drag-and-drop for individual split panes. Drag a pane by its header outside the window to open it in its own window, or drop it on a terminal in another window of the same Pebrel instance to dock it. Press Alt during the drag to open a new window from a maximized layout. The running session and scrollback move with the pane.

## 简体中文

### 新增

- 支持直接拖动单个分屏。抓住分屏标题条拖出窗口即可拆成独立窗口，也可拖入同一 Pebrel 实例的另一个终端窗口并入分屏。窗口最大化时，可在拖动中按 Alt 拆出新窗口。正在运行的会话和滚动历史随分屏一起移动。
